Marcus, 34, is eight weeks into parole supervision in Denver after a three-year CDOC sentence. He works a 10 PM–6 AM warehouse shift (the only job he could get quickly), lives with his brother, who has a felony record (his only housing option at release), and has a 9 PM–6 AM curfew. The parole office is only open Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

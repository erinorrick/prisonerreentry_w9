Week 9 Engagement Activity

Supervision Condition Analysis

Below are six conditions drawn from real, commonly used state supervision practice. For each one, decide (1) whether it primarily serves risk management, and (2) whether it risks creating a barrier to reintegration — housing, employment, or treatment. Then make a recommendation: retain, modify, or eliminate, with a brief justification. When you're done, use "Compare to the evidence" to check your reasoning against what the research literature actually says about that condition type.

This page is a working space and does not save your work. Copy your six ratings and recommendations into the submission document linked on the Week 9 assignment page.

Working Definitions

Term What it means here
Risk management The condition exists to monitor or limit behavior directly tied to reoffending risk.
Reintegration barrier The condition makes it harder to keep a job, housing, or treatment appointment — regardless of whether it also manages risk.

Note: a condition can do both, or neither, at the same time. That overlap is the point of this activity.
